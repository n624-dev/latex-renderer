# Reviewing the final PDF

A successful compile establishes that TeX produced output. Record the remaining checks separately.

1. **Diagnostics.** Read errors and warnings, including font substitution, missing glyphs, unresolved references, bibliography problems, and text outside its box. Use the diagnostic page cursor and log-range operation if available. Read any unreturned sections needed to identify the problem.
2. **Pages.** Get the declared page count from the PDF. Inspect every page of the final output. Check hierarchy, clipping, spacing, blank areas, and page breaks. Zoom in when subscripts, thin lines, or table labels are too small. After a change that affects pagination, check the affected pages again.
3. **Content.** Match signs, bounds, subscripts, variable names, table values, graph labels, and units to the source. Check that required material is present and excluded material stays excluded. Keep ambiguous source material explicit.
4. **Delivery.** Record the saved revision, Job, and PDF digest when available. Supply the PDF from that Job. Identify uninspected pages, warnings accepted by the user, and any content you could not verify.

An intentional blank area, a ragged paragraph, or a font that is present but unused may be appropriate. Assess the actual document and the user's requirements.
